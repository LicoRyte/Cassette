# Solarist: traits, workshop, ship page and more content

Asked for: a second trait, a research tech tree with more in it, a crafting page (ship equipment, accessories and
more) with a list of what you've made, a cleaner ship customization page, and more content everywhere so the
stories go more places.

## Phases

| Phase | What | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ✅ | **Traits**: everyone can have one or two traits; 12 new traits with their own lines | Pick two traits in the crew editor; stories notice both |
| 2 ✅ | **Workshop**: three tabs. **Research** is a tech tree (branches as columns, each upgrade needing the ones above it, about twice as many upgrades). **Crafting** makes a chosen thing from blueprints that research unlocks (clothes by slot, equipment, ship equipment), plus a cheap "surprise me". **Made** lists everything you've made and every blueprint you know | Research opens blueprints; crafting picks exactly what you make |
| 3 ✅ | **Ship page**: the cutaway on the left; on the right, the picked room's settings as rows with arrows (Wall ◂ Steel panels ▸), like the crew editor; hull paint the same way. No "use here" cards, no counters, no extra notes | Decorating takes one or two clicks per change |
| 4 ✅ | **Content**: crew stories (a new kind of storyline: one crew member's own arc over several sessions, picked by who they are), and more wonders, mysteries, traders, activities, skies, deep-space events, ship moments and crew pair moments | Every list is noticeably longer; crew stories show in the Journal and the crew editor |

Nothing is a number you grind: research opens blueprints, stories and small perks; crafting costs Knowledge points,
which come from studying.
