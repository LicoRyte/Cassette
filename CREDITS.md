# Credits

Cassie is a non-commercial personal project. Thanks to the people whose work it uses:

- **Colour palette:** a 32-colour palette by **BurakoIRL**, used for everything.
- **Fonts:** [Barlow / Barlow Condensed](https://github.com/jpt/barlow) by Jeremy Tribby [Varela Round](https://github.com/alefalefalef/Varela-Round-Hebrew) by Joe Prince, and [Kodchasan](https://github.com/cadsondemak/Kodchasan) by Cadson Demak (the cozy font choice in Settings), all under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/`).
- **Sounds:** see `sfx/CREDITS.txt` (the clock tick by virtual_vibes on Pixabay, interface sounds from the Not-So-Awesome Archive, and the cassette key). The chimes and desk sounds are made live in the browser.
- **Radio:** the station directory is [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info), free and community-run. Each station's sound belongs to that station.

Everything else (code, writing, the icon) was made for Cassie.
