# Credits

Solarist is a non-commercial personal project. Thanks to the people whose work it uses:

- **Colour palette:** a 32-colour palette by **BurakoIRL**, used for everything (planets, interface, ship and effects). The colours are listed in `docs/ui-redesign-plan.md`. Earlier versions used [Apollo](https://lospec.com/palette-list/apollo) by AdamCYounis and [Resurrect 64](https://lospec.com/palette-list/resurrect-64) by Kerrie Lake.
- **Planet shader:** the planet looks (lit colour bands, land masses, clouds, rings, craters, lava and rivers, gas bands) are ported from [PixelPlanets](https://github.com/Deep-Fold/PixelPlanets) by **Deep-Fold**, MIT License (see `js/LICENSE-PixelPlanets.txt`).
- **Fonts:** [Barlow / Barlow Condensed](https://github.com/jpt/barlow) by Jeremy Tribby and [Courier Prime](https://quoteunquoteapps.com/courierprime/) by Alan Dague-Greene, both under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/`).
- **Sounds:** see `sfx/CREDITS.txt` (session sounds by NightMargin, interface sounds from the Not-So-Awesome Archive).
- **Music:** see `music/CREDITS.txt` ("Night Stars" by Hiroki971 plays during breaks).

Everything else (code, writing, pixel art) was made for Solarist.
